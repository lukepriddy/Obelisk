Why the first zone used to fire in the wrong place

Here is something people find out the hard way when they build a walk. A phone does not know where it is. It guesses, and the guess gets better over the first few seconds.

When someone opens a walk and starts moving straight away, that first guess can be off by a long way. Wide open field, clear sky, no buildings? Maybe fifteen feet. City street with tall buildings on both sides? It can be a hundred feet or worse, and it will wander before it settles.

What that does to a walk

If your first zone is small, the walk breaks before it starts. Either nothing plays when the player is standing in the right place, or something plays while they are still in the car park.

Neither of those reads as a GPS problem to the person it happens to. It reads as a broken experience, and they blame you.

What we do about it

Obelisk does not show the map straight away. It holds a screen in front of the walk while the signal settles, and shows a ring that tightens as the reading gets better. When the reading is good enough, the walk begins.

It takes a few seconds. Most people are still putting their headphones in. Nobody has ever complained about it, and the first zone now fires where it should.

A second thing, quieter

Even after the signal settles, it still drifts a little. So a zone is slightly harder to leave than it is to enter. If you have ever stood still and watched an app decide you had walked away, that is the problem this fixes.

You will not notice either of these when they work. That is rather the point.
