Walk into a story.

Obelisk lets you tie sound and characters to real places. Someone walks to the spot, and that part of the story plays on their phone. There is no app to install.

Start building

What it is

A map, and things that happen on it.

You pick spots on a map and decide what each one does. One spot might play a voice. Another might hide a code someone has to work out. Another might be a character they can talk to.

Then you share a link. People open it in their browser and start walking. Nothing happens until they reach a spot. That is the whole idea.

How it works

Three steps.

1

Mark your spots

Drop pins on a map where you want something to happen. Make them as small or as wide as you like.

2

Say what happens

Add audio, write a character, or lock a spot behind a password people have to find first.

3

Share the link

Send it to anyone. They walk, their phone listens for the spots, and the story plays itself.

Built for the real world

It waits until it knows where you are.

Phones guess your location, and the guess gets better over the first few seconds. Most apps show you a map right away and let the dot jump around. Obelisk waits for the signal to settle before the walk begins, so the first spot plays where it should.

This is the screen players see while their phone finds them.
