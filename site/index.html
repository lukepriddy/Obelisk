Zones

A zone is a spot on the map with a size. Whatever you put there starts when someone walks in.

Any size Loop, play once, or play once ever Hide it from the map Fade in and out Quieter near the edge Require another zone first

Voices

Type your script and pick a voice. ElevenLabs and Google voices are built in, so you do not have to record anything.

ElevenLabs Over 20 voices Notes on accent and pace Or upload your own audio

AI characters

You write who they are and what they are hiding. Players type questions and get answers back in that voice.

Typed conversation Something they hold back Stays in character Can open a locked zone
You said it. A name said out loud is a door. What is under the water? Ask me that again when you have walked further.

Locked zones

Put a password on a zone and hide the answer earlier in the walk. A character can open one too, once they decide it is earned.

Passwords Hints you write Opened by a character Answers checked on the server

Collectibles

Hide items along the route for players to pick up. A zone can require one before it opens.

Hidden items On-screen counter Require items to enter Hidden until collected

AR objects

Place an object at a spot and it appears when a player points their camera there. Still or flying.

Camera objects Static or flying Placed on the map No install

GPS accuracy

A phone's location is a guess that gets better over the first few seconds. Obelisk waits for it to settle before the walk starts, so the first zone does not fire in the wrong place.

This is the screen players see while their phone finds them.
