About

Places already hold stories.

Obelisk is a tool for putting one back.

Why it exists

A tour on your phone usually means reading a screen while you stand in front of something. You look down instead of up. The place becomes a backdrop.

Walking works better when you listen. Your eyes stay on the street, the water, the tree, the building. The story arrives when you get there, and then you keep moving.

That is what Obelisk is for. Not a guide that follows you around, but a story that waits in the right spot until you find it.

Who it is for

Writers. Teachers. Museums and parks. Anyone running a game night, a history walk, or a treasure hunt for their neighbourhood.

You do not need to code. You do not need a team. If you can write and drop a pin on a map, you can make one.

How it is built

Everything runs in a browser. Nobody has to download an app to walk your experience, and you do not have to wait on an app store to change it.

Voices and characters use AI, which you can pay for with your own key or leave to us. What people hear at each spot is written by you.

Where it is up to

Obelisk is new and still small. It is being built in the open by one person, and it is invite only for now while the rough edges get sorted.

If you want to make something with it, say hello at hello@obelisk.place.
